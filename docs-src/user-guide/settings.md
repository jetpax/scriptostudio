# Settings

Click the gear at the top of the menu to open **Settings**. It lists About, Appearance, Agent and Firmware, then under **Networks** the panels the board's hardware calls for. The gear stays lit while Settings shows; click the house to go back to the Apps.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Device**, which follows your computer or phone, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Accent**: the colour of what's chosen and what to press.

Changes apply at once.

## Networks

Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Mobile data, VPN, MQTT, Telephony, Time and CAN, as the board has them. See [Network Setup](../device-setup/network-setup.md).
